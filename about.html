About FarmPulse

Building a smarter, more resilient agricultural future through accessible data and intelligent advisory.

Our Mission

FarmPulse was created with a singular goal: to empower farmers with data-driven insights. By combining real-time meteorological data with established agronomic rules, we provide actionable recommendations that help reduce risk and improve yield.

We believe that modern AgriTech should not be complicated. It should be intuitive, fast, and accessible directly from the field.

Platform Pillars

  • 1
    Data Accuracy: Utilizing Open-Meteo for precise hyper-local forecasting.
  • 2
    Agronomic Soundness: Recommendations based on generalized crop science rules.
  • 3
    Accessibility: Fast, offline-capable, and designed for rural connectivity.

Data Sources & Transparency

FarmPulse uses open data and transparent rule-based engines. We do not use "black-box" AI that provides unexplainable advice.

🌤️ Weather Data

Live weather, soil moisture, and 7-day forecasts are fetched securely from the Open-Meteo API.

🌱 Crop Intelligence

Crop suitability scores are generated using our internal rule-engine matching temperature, season, soil, and water requirements.

💾 Local Privacy

Your farm details, location, and tasks are stored entirely on your device via LocalStorage. We do not collect or track your data.

Disclaimer:

FarmPulse provides informational estimates based on available data and user inputs. The application does not provide guaranteed agricultural, financial, or AI predictions. Actual farming decisions should always consider local, on-the-ground soil conditions, specific crop varieties, expert advice from local agronomists, and direct field observations.
